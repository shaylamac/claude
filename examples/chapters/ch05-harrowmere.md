# Chapter Five

Queen Maren stood on the walls of Harrowmere. Her green eyes caught the light.

Lady Maren had never liked the Glass Sea at dusk.

Tavish Rook bowed low. He had come from Saltspyre with news.

Corin Vale laughed. His blue eyes were bright with mischief.

Maren was twenty-five years old that winter, and her brother, Corin was already restless.

Maren's mother, Tavish Rook, said nothing.

The saltspire keep was silent. The tide-caller waited.
